My Mathematical Journey.

I never thought of myself as a math person. For most of my life, it was the subject that felt the hardest and the least rewarding, so I stayed with it mostly because of the opportunities it could open up. College changed that. For the first time, I had professors whose passion for mathematics made it feel alive, and that pushed me to take more rigorous classes. Over time, what started as a practical choice became something much deeper: an appreciation for the beauty, structure, and meaning of the subject itself. Mathematics has become a lens through which I understand the world. Here is how I understand my mathematical journey up to now:

    What the interactive means

    A radial vector-space metaphor

    Related subjects point in nearby directions, basis vectors stay closer to the origin, and later subjects appear either as scalar extensions or as combinations of earlier directions. Conceptual points such as Complex Numbers and Optimization explain why some branches bend away rather than continue collinearly.

    Basis vectors
    Discrete Math, Linear Algebra, and Multivariable Calculus are the chosen basis directions of the interactive.
    Analysis branch
    Real Analysis is built from foundations, Measure Theory extends it, and Complex Analysis branches away through the added conceptual direction of Complex Numbers.
    Learning branch
    Probability anchors the branch, Mathematical Statistics extends it collinearly, and Statistical Learning branches away through the added conceptual direction of Optimization.
    Scientific Applications
    Differential Equations represents the applied side of the map, where continuous mathematics is used to model dynamics, systems, and scientific change.
    What this span currently means

    My current mathematical span is organized around four interacting branches: discrete and algebraic structure, the analysis sequence, probability/statistics/learning, and scientific applications. Together, they support both my theoretical and applied interests in machine learning by combining proof, abstraction, representation, uncertainty, inference, optimization, and modeling into one coherent mathematical foundation.
